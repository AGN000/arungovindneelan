Services

What I Can Do For You

End-to-end consulting across simulation, physics-informed AI, and scientific software — delivered with research-grade rigour

🌏

CFD Consulting & Simulation

High-fidelity flow simulations using OpenFOAM, ANSYS Fluent, or custom in-house solvers. RANS/LES turbulence, shock-capturing (WENO, AUSM+), compressible & incompressible regimes. Deliverable: validated simulation results and a documented workflow your team can own.

🤖

AI Agent Development for Engineering

Custom offline AI agents that automate repetitive simulation workflows — case setup, meshing, solver configuration, log-parsing — using LLMs with RAG on your internal docs. Runs locally on standard hardware. Deliverable: a working agent integrated with your toolchain.

🤘

Custom Solver & Numerical Software

From-scratch or OpenFOAM-extended PDE solvers tailored to your physics: high-order WENO, implicit Krylov methods, GPU-ready JAX/PyFR implementations. I build, validate, and hand over documented, tested code — not just a prototype.

⛅️

AI Weather & Climate Modelling

Diffusion-based downscaling (CorrDiff), GNN spatiotemporal forecasting (GraphCast), physics-guided latent dynamics. I can set up, fine-tune, and benchmark these pipelines on your regional dataset. Deliverable: a reproducible training + inference stack.

🧫

Physics-Informed AI & Surrogate Models

PINNs for forward and inverse problems, reduced-order models (POD, DMD, ANN surrogates), denoising and reconstruction from noisy experimental data. Cut your high-fidelity simulation budget by 10–100× with validated surrogates.

🛠

Aerospace & High-Speed Flow Analysis

Scramjet combustion, shock-induced mixing, spiked combustor and forebody shape optimisation, re-entry aerodynamics, Euler adjoint design loops. Over a decade of published results in these areas backing every engagement.
